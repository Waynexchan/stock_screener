## Hypothesis

MODEL_0's large capacity-limited drawdown may be reduced by limiting simultaneous initial risk and by earning the right to expand exposure. The trade signal, entry, initial stop, no-target exit and 40-session maximum hold remain frozen; only portfolio allocation changes.

Use an abstract starting account of 100R, equal to USD 58,700 at the fixed USD 587 standard R. Calculate daily mark-to-market equity using only prices known on each session. Maximum drawdown percent is measured from the running high-water equity, not by dividing all losses by today's private account balance.

## Fixed variants

Test fixed 1R, 2R, 3R and 4R maximum initial heat. Test two earned-exposure ladders that start at 2R and permanently unlock 3R/4R after realised-profit high-water marks of +1R/+2R or +2R/+4R. Test drawdown overlays at 2R/4R/6R and 3R/6R/9R, plus the conservative earned-exposure and 2R/4R/6R combination. A position continues to consume its allocated initial R until exit; same-session exits cannot fund a new entry earlier that day.

The 2R/4R/6R overlay mirrors the current production concept: normal risk, then half-risk/reduced heat, then half-risk/defensive heat, then stop new risk. This is a calibration experiment, not evidence that current thresholds are optimal. To prevent 40-session labels crossing into the reserved period, the last eligible signal date is 2023-11-01 and every exit must occur by 2023-12-29.

Report all variants. The current-symbol archive is survivorship-biased, there is no cash/notional cap, and 2024–2025 remains untouched; therefore no result can directly change production.
